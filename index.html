Who I am

More than just a 16-year-old

Hey — I'm Graham. A 16-year-old creator and builder from Chennai, India. I run a YouTube channel about aviation, I produce music, and I'm learning to code my way into a full-stack developer career. I'm a left-handed, creative-minded guy — I see the world a little differently, and I think that shows in everything I make.

I love planes — spotting them, filming them, and sharing that passion through Visible Aviation on YouTube. If there's a flight overhead, I already noticed it before you did.

I'm also a music producer working in Logic Pro, and I handle lighting for live productions using MagicQ, Capture, and GrandMA3. I also edit video in Final Cut Pro and stream with OBS.

My faith in Jesus keeps me grounded through all of it. I'm a follower of Jesus, willing to serve God through church ministries — prayer teams, eGroups, and worship. Everything I build is for something bigger than myself.
